## Change to make

File to edit: Directory.Build.props (covers every project under it) — or any one consuming project's .csproj.

Add the properties below to a <PropertyGroup>. One declaration covers every package from owner 'acme'; the property names are exact (the owner prefix is baked into the package).

```xml
<PropertyGroup>
  <acme_SponsorshipExemption>Consulting</acme_SponsorshipExemption>
  <acme_SponsorshipExemptionUntil>2027-02</acme_SponsorshipExemptionUntil>
</PropertyGroup>
```

## Expected build outcome

Passes with a SC031 warning quoting the publisher's criteria for 'Consulting': "Consulting clients are exempt for 6 months." — the build log records the specific carve-out being claimed rather than a generic breach message. The publisher time-bounds this exemption to 6 months, so SponsorshipExemptionUntil is required — omitting it fails with SC040, and a month more than 6 past the build clock fails with SC046. It passes through the end of 2027-02 (UTC); after that the build fails with SC049, which is the point: the claim has to be re-checked rather than left in place.

Notes:

- The properties cover every package from owner 'acme' — one declaration for the whole family. The exact property names are also printed in the SC021 error message.
